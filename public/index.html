FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before we
get started.

Every vehicle and every brief is different. You are welcome to get in touch with an early idea.

Can I get in touch with just an idea or a sketch?

Yes. Tell us about your vehicle, who will use it, the spaces you like and the problems you want to solve. Any sketches or existing information will help the conversation.

What will the design include?

Depending on the brief, it may cover layout, furniture, service points and visualisations. Drawing types, level of detail and file formats are agreed before work begins.

How are fees, timing and revisions decided?

These depend on the vehicle information available, the scope and complexity. Once we understand your needs, we can agree on a fee, schedule and revision scope.

Can the drawings be used directly for construction?

The images and drawings shown here are concepts, not construction documents. The depth of actual project drawings must be agreed separately; construction, equipment safety and vehicle compliance should be verified by the relevant specialists and build team.

Can you coordinate with my own build team?

Tell us about your build team and the coordination you need when you get in touch. The approach and scope are agreed for each project.
