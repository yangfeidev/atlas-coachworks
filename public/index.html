09 — Questions

Asked before
every contract.

These are the eight things buyers actually ask on the first call. If yours is not here, it will be answered within one working day.

Ask something else
What is the minimum order quantity?
30 units for a turnkey OEM programme, 20 on a licensed platform, 60 for build-to-print. Below those numbers the tooling amortisation stops making sense for both sides — we will say so in the feasibility letter rather than sell you a bad programme.
Who owns the tooling and the design?
You do. Tooling is invoiced separately and its ownership transfers on payment, with a physical handover of the moulds or a buy-back clause if you move production. Design IP is assigned to you at gate 03. We keep no right to resell your design, your graphics or your floor plan — and we will sign a no-compete for your market.
Will you build for a competitor in the same country?
Not on the same platform, and not without telling you. Territory exclusivity is available from 150 units a year on a single platform. For smaller programmes we disclose every active client in your market before gate 02 — you decide with the facts in hand.
What does a prototype cost?
€45,000–90,000 for two running prototypes, depending on platform and systems complexity. At 100+ units the prototype cost is credited against your first series invoice. A clay model alone, before any engineering, is €12,000–18,000.
What are the payment terms?
30% at tooling release, 40% at pre-series sign-off, 30% against the bill of lading. Letter of credit at sight is accepted for a first order. We do not ask for full prepayment on series units, and we do not ship against open account in year one.
Can we audit the factory?
Yes — announced or unannounced, any working day. BSCI and Sedex social audit reports are available under NDA before you travel. Your engineers can stay on the floor through a full build cycle; we will put them in the line meeting too.
How long does a programme take?
14–18 months from brief to start of production on a new platform. 5–7 months if you start from an existing homologated platform. Build-to-print is 3 months of onboarding, then the line cycle applies — 12 to 26 days per unit depending on model.
What warranty do you give?
Five years structural, two years habitation, held by the plant rather than a distributor. A parts pool sits in Rotterdam for European customers; we ship spares within 5 working days and support your dealer network directly during the warranty period.
